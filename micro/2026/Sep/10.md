# Everything Will Change After This Comment, and I Can't Wait to Be There Together

It's time to start the substantive drafting I've been waiting three years for. SORRY if you were relying on me for something or needed an answer on things. I will be pretty much available for the next two months.

I really can't thank the community enough for getting me here. There is ZERO chance I could do what I'm about to do without your support. Namely, the free capital donations (in fiscal and bandwidth and commitment and just so much more) really let me be me.

With all the early venture capitalists, all the initial pitching, and all the "startup" expectations of my structured early mentorship environments, I was basically always masking. I could never really talk about what I cared about because nobody cared about these weird FTD things. Even the people in industry I talked to for so long saw nothing wrong with the fiber cables routing to market makers next door to the exchange.

And I suppose it got to me over the years. Selling something with half my heart because of the impossible complexities to explain... well, it just wasn't very effective. And I always knew it couldn't reach full intent because I needed all of the shares to move into a nonprofit anyway.

And so I turned to grants as a big source of opportunity, once I realized I couldn't do what I wanted in the trading markets. Over the years I met some great people and came to really enjoy their company. It's gut-wrenching to then see their structures reject you over and over again for the same reasons Sand Hill cited.

I've talked about the hardest parts before, and I won't reiterate. It all almost ended until I found the community. You guys showed me the support and friendship I needed to finally let down my walls from those formative years—and I'll always be thankful for that.

I started noticing it when I could say really out-there stuff while keeping my GitHub sponsorships. We could have civil and productive disagreements without changing the fundamental trust and collaboration. That was two years ago, and since then I've found the confidence to express my real intents and motives thanks to everyone.
